# Qualify the workflow

Confirm these facts before quoting the implementation:

- The business has an actual recurring scan/rename/file problem.
- Documents already carry a stable routable business identifier.
- Each incoming file represents one business document.
- An owner or authorized decision-maker can approve the system.
- The site has a supported Windows machine and an existing scanner/drop-folder path.
- The buyer can supply redacted examples with known correct filing IDs.
- The buyer can measure daily volume, manual handling time and correction/retrieval effort.
- The requested outcome fits one site and one workflow without an ERP integration dependency.

Keep unknown facts unknown. A business category, website screenshot or assumed revenue does not establish workflow fit. Enterprise purchasing cycles and operators without buying authority are excluded from the initial offer.

Calculate time savings from observed workflow data. Do not promise a specific ROI, percentage accuracy or throughput from synthetic tests.
