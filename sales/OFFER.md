# BarcodeBuddy Document Automation System

Price: $10,000 per site, paid once. This number is David's to confirm before it goes to a buyer.

For an owner-operated packaging, distribution, manufacturing or supply business that spends time scanning, renaming, filing and retrieving barcoded paperwork.

## What the buyer gets

One physical site and one accepted document workflow: Windows installation, customer-specific barcode rules, scanner-folder intake, deterministic PDF filing, explicit rejected-document review, operator/admin setup, sample acceptance, verified backup recovery, handoff documentation and a 30-day stabilization window.

## Who owns what

The buyer pays once and keeps a perpetual license to run the delivered version at their site. The full terms are in LICENSE-AGREEMENT.md.

- The buyer owns their installed copy, their documents, their database, their configuration and their backups. The system runs on their own computer and keeps working whether or not they buy support.
- David keeps ownership of the software and the right to license it to other businesses. The buyer may not resell it, share it, or run it for other companies.
- Support after the 30-day window is optional and separate. Ending support never ends the license.

## Not included

ERP attachment integration, OCR/handwriting, signature verification, mixed-document splitting, custom mobile capture, multi-site deployment and new customer-specific features. Extra workflows or sites need a separate written agreement and their own license.

## What proves what

Technical release readiness, a signed agreement, payment and customer acceptance are separate facts. A reference receipt proves the tested product revision; it does not prove a sale or buyer deployment.
